276	About Rifqat al-Uswa
277	How does it work?
278	The app analyses what you write locally on your device: it detects the topics, feelings and the relationship involved, then retrieves from a verified library (situations from the Seerah and the stories of the Prophets, verses, and hadith with their grades) the texts closest to your situation, and builds a reading and a practical plan from them.
279	Privacy:
280	What you write is never sent to any server and is not saved. The only exception: if you enable “Deepen with AI” with your own key, your text and the retrieved passages are sent directly to Claude (Anthropic) to generate the analysis.
281	Scholarly integrity:
282	The app never generates verses or hadith; every text is taken from the library as it is. English meanings of the Quran are from Saheeh International (via Tanzil.net), and English hadith translations follow the published sunnah.com translations. Verses are shown in standard spelling; refer to the Mushaf when reciting.
283	This is a reflective, guiding app; it is not a substitute for the fatwa of a qualified scholar, or for consulting a mental-health or legal professional when needed.
